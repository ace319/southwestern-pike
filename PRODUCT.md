# Product context

## Surface
Public brand and recruitment website for the Alpha Omicron Chapter of Pi Kappa Alpha at Southwestern University.

## Audiences
Prospective members, current members, parents, alumni, campus partners, and the Georgetown community.

## Goals
Communicate Alpha Omicron's identity and history, support ethical recruitment, showcase scholarship and service, reconnect alumni, and provide clear public contact routes.

## Voice
Confident, warm, grounded, inclusive, and specific to Southwestern. Avoid generic fraternity bravado and unsupported claims.

## Constraints
Do not publish private member information, outdated officer names, internal chapter procedures, or changing claims without chapter confirmation.
