# Design direction

Collegiate modernism with Texas warmth. Garnet and old gold lead the palette; cream paper and tinted near-black provide contrast. Bold, condensed varsity typography creates a recruitment-poster voice, while Work Sans keeps longer copy direct and readable.

The experience favors architectural rulework, shield geometry, offset yearbook layouts, bold chapter numerals, and purposeful motion. Avoid the oversized literary-serif language and linear atlas structure used by the separate Albatross project. Also avoid generic card grids, gray text on colored backgrounds, excessive rounding, and decorative effects without meaning.

Photography should feel candid and chapter-specific: the house, service, scholarship, recruitment, athletics, alumni, and genuine brotherhood. Replace the initial portrait or expand the gallery when the chapter supplies approved high-resolution photographs.
