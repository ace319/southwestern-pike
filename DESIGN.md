# Design direction

Editorial heritage with contemporary Texas warmth. Garnet and old gold lead the palette; cream paper and tinted near-black provide contrast. Newsreader supplies expressive historical typography, while Manrope and DM Mono handle contemporary body copy and labels.

The experience favors strong hierarchy, open spacing, full-width bands, restrained borders, and purposeful motion. Avoid generic card grids, gray text on colored backgrounds, excessive rounding, and decorative effects without meaning.

Photography should feel candid and chapter-specific: the house, service, scholarship, recruitment, athletics, alumni, and genuine brotherhood. Replace the initial portrait or expand the gallery when the chapter supplies approved high-resolution photographs.
